# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, give actionable feedback, respect different backgrounds and levels of experience, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other project spaces. The repository owner may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct is unsafe or disruptive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled with appropriate confidentiality and without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
